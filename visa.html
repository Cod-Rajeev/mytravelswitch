Essential Document Checklist

Documents You May Need

Keep these commonly requested documents and details ready. The exact list depends on the country and visa type.

Valid Passport

Essential

Check passport validity, blank pages, personal details and condition according to your destination's requirements.

Recent Passport-Size Photograph

Prepare a clear photograph that follows the required size, background, lighting and digital upload specifications.

Visa Application Details

Keep your personal information, contact details, travel purpose and other requested application details accurate.

Travel Itinerary & Accommodation

Prepare proposed travel dates, flight itinerary, hotel details or accommodation information where required.

Financial Supporting Documents

Bank statements, income proof, sponsorship documents or other financial evidence, if requested.

Additional Supporting Documents

Employment letters, business proof, invitation letters, admission documents or other supporting paperwork as applicable.

One Important Thing Before You Apply

Visa policies, document requirements, fees, appointment procedures and processing times can change. Always verify the latest information through the destination country's official embassy, consulate or immigration website before applying. Keep copies of your submitted documents and check your approved visa carefully before travelling.
